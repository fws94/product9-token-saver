# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome participation regardless of background, identity or experience. This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces maintained for Token Saver.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence; avoid personal attacks.
- Be constructive when reporting defects or disagreeing with a design.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Allow newcomers room to learn, and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, doxxing and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For abusive GitHub content, use GitHub's **Report content** action. For a non-sensitive moderation request, contact a [maintainer](GOVERNANCE.md) through an appropriate existing project thread. Do not repeat private or abusive material in a public report. No dedicated private conduct-reporting mailbox is currently provided; use GitHub's private abuse-reporting route for sensitive reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation or involve GitHub's moderation team. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Disagreements with moderation should be raised respectfully; avoid creating duplicate disruptive threads.
